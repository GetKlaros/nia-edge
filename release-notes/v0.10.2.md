Release of nia-edge, the client of Nia. It is a pre-release.

**Added**
- **A payment the vault refused shows in Nia's live view.** When the vault refuses the agent's `prepare` or `authorize` (over the allowance, a recipient the rule does not name, a reference already used), nia-edge tells Nia the NEAR transaction. Nia records it only once NEAR shows that transaction failing, and shows it as the vault's refusal. Telling Nia never delays the agent's answer, and failing to stops nothing.

**Upgrade from 0.10.1**

`sudo sh update-linux.sh v0.10.2`, then `sudo -iu hermes hermes gateway restart`. A Nia of 23 November 2026 or later is required.
