Release of nia-edge, the client of Nia. It is a pre-release.

**Added**
- **Undo a change from Nia's console.** nia-edge reports the last changes nia-hostd can undo (an apply, a package installed) with each heartbeat. A person who presses **Undo** on the agent's card in the console gets the change undone at the machine's next heartbeat: Nia signs the order for this machine (over its own domain, `nia-undo/1`, ten minutes at most), nia-hostd verifies it itself with the key pinned at enrollment and runs it once. nia-edge only passes the order and reports what it did.

**Upgrade from 0.12.0**

`sudo sh update-linux.sh v0.13.0`. Needs a Nia of 25 November 2026 or later, which says it takes the report of changes; an older Nia sends no order.
