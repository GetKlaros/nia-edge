Release of nia-edge, the client of Nia. It is a pre-release.

**Added**
- **A change that breaks the machine comes back by itself.** After an apply or a package install, nia-hostd checks that what answered before still answers, within 30 seconds: ssh on `127.0.0.1:22`, the catalogue's active services, and the new `health` checks of the catalogue (an http(s) URL or a port, on the loopback only). Otherwise the former files come back, or the package is removed, and the journal says what no longer answered.
- **An apply reloads the services it reconfigured** (nginx, apache2, haproxy, when they are in the catalogue and were active), so the health checks see what the change did. Undoing an apply reloads them again.

**Upgrade from 0.11.0**

`sudo sh update-linux.sh v0.12.0`. A role whose catalogue names `health` needs this nia-edge; an older one refuses such a catalogue whole.
